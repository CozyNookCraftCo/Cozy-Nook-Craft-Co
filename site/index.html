Our Story

From a spare bedroom to your keepsake box.

Cozy Nook Craft Co. began with a single roll of craft paper wrapping. Today it's grown into a little world for book lovers, but the heart of it hasn't changed. Everything is still made, wrapped, and packed by hand.

What started with paper has grown into everything a cozy reader could want. Blind Date Books wrapped by genre, so all you know until you tear it open is the mood inside. Folded book art that turns a favorite story into a keepsake. Soy wax melts that smell like your favorite chapters. Crochet pieces, bookish gifts, and boxes made for slow, snuggled-up nights. Every order comes with handcrafted little touches that make opening it feel like a gift.

We make each piece with love, for the reader who deserves a cozy moment with something they never saw coming.

📖 Origin Story

Sheri's journey began with a single roll of craft paper. One wrapped book became two, then a whole world of Blind Dates with Books, each wrapped by genre so all you know is the mood inside. The nook grew from a spare bedroom into a little world for readers, but every order is still made, wrapped and packed by hand, often live on TikTok with the whole community cheering along.

📚 Favorite Reads

Twisted villain retellings, spine-tingling thrillers, dragon-filled romantasy and swoony holiday rom-coms. There's never a bad time to curl up with a good book!

🎀 Aesthetic

Blush pink, soft rose and gold, pressed flowers and sprayed edges, flickering candlelight and moonlit windows, chunky knit blankets, a warm mug of something sweet, and stacks of old books tucked into a cozy reading nook.

💕 Personality

Warm, generous and full of ideas. Can't resist a gorgeous special edition, loves turning simple materials into one-of-a-kind treasures, and always tucks a little extra goodie into every order.
